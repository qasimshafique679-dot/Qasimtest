### Why it works
- **The pull-back reveal:** it opens on a mystery (a hand on strange skin), and the scale gets bigger every second.
- **The emotional mirror:** human tear → dragon tear. Viewers feel there's a story without a single word.
- **10 seconds with no text** loops well, so people rewatch it and watch time goes up.
