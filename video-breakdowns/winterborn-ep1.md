# Winterborn — Episode 1: "The Maid in the Snow"

- **Format:** vertical 9:16 teaser, ~28 s, 11 shots
- **Genre:** hidden heiress / revenge family drama
- **Setting:** snow-covered alpine mansion (Western, winter night)
- **Tools:** generic prompts that work in Veo, Kling and Hailuo
- **Logline:** On the night of the Winter family's Christmas gala, a mistreated maid gets thrown out into the snow. Then the family lawyer arrives and kneels to her as the true heir to the Winter fortune.

---

## 1. Story hook

| Beat | Time | What happens |
|------|------|--------------|
| **Hook** | 0–3 s | Stepsister throws a glass of champagne in Elena's face: "A maid doesn't belong at this party!" |
| **Pressure** | 3–12 s | Stepmother orders her into the snow; Elena is pushed out the front doors, kneeling in the snow |
| **Twist** | 12–22 s | Black car arrives; old lawyer Arthur steps out, kneels, opens a briefcase: "Miss Winter… your father left everything to you." |
| **Cliffhanger** | 22–28 s | Stepmother's champagne glass slips and shatters; close-up on Elena's cold, rising gaze. Cut to black + CTA |
