## 1. Story hook

- **0–2 s hook:** a storm-grey dragon the size of a house lands right in front of a lone man. Rocks shatter and he doesn't run.
- **Twist:** the dragon is hunting the man's village. It lowers its head to strike, sees the old burn mark on his palm, and **bows**. It recognises him as the rider it lost.
- **Cliffhanger:** far off, a second roar echoes back. The man's face shows fear, because he knows that one *isn't* friendly. Cut to black: "Full episodes in bio".
