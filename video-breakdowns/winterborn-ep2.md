# Winterborn — Episode 2: "Pour Me a Glass"

- **Format:** vertical 9:16 teaser, ~30 s, 12 shots
- **Genre:** hidden heiress / revenge family drama (continues from Ep 1)
- **Setting:** the same Winter mansion ballroom, Christmas night, minutes after Ep 1
- **Tools:** generic prompts that work in Veo, Kling and Hailuo
- **Logline:** Elena walks back into the gala as the heir and makes her stepsister serve her. Vivian calls the will a fake, but Arthur has one more document, and it suggests Elena's father's death was no accident.

## 7. CapCut edit plan

1. Put clips 1–12 in order and trim them to the shot-list timing (~30 s), plus a 2 s end card.
2. Optional **recap flash (0.5 s)** before Shot 1: Ep 1's Shot 1 (the champagne splash) with a white flash transition, so new viewers get the context.
3. **Audio:** cut the party music dead with a record scratch on Shot 1. Use a slow, confident beat (taiko or low strings) for 3–13 s, rising tension for 13–21 s, then a silent beat on Shot 10 before the strings sting on Shot 11 and a deep boom on Shot 12. SFX: doors boom, murmurs, glass clink, pour and fizz, heels, paper rustle, wax seal crack.
4. **Auto-captions:** white bold, centre lower-third. Highlight in yellow: *maid*, *pour*, *fake*, *nobody*, *my father*, *died*, *accident*.
5. **Title bar** at the top: "WINTERBORN · EP 2" in bold white with an icy-blue outline, plus the same ❄️ sticker.
6. **Name tag** on Shot 1: "Elena Winter — Heir to the Winter Fortune". The changed tag from Ep 1 is part of the payoff.
7. **Bottom CTA:** "Full Episodes in bio 👇" with an animated arrow, from Shot 10 to the end.
8. Grade: keep the ballroom warm and add a cool-blue tint to Shot 12 to signal danger.
