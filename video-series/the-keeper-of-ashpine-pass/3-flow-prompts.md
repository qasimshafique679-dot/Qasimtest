# The Keeper of Ashpine Pass — Stage 3: Google Flow Video Prompts

**8 clips × 8 s · 16:9 · Veo in Google Flow**

### How to use each clip
1. In Flow, open your project and attach the **Ingredients** listed for that clip (your Gemini pictures).
2. From clip 2 onward, if Flow offers **Frames to Video**, use the **last frame of the previous clip** as the first frame, so the clips join smoothly.
3. Paste the **FLOW PROMPT** exactly as written. Generate 2–3 takes and keep the best.
4. If an action gets cut off, use **Extend** on the best take rather than rewriting the prompt.

*Clips 1, 2 and 4 are a little longer than 180 words because three or more characters are in the frame, and every locked line must be repeated word for word.*

## Quick fixes if a take goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| A face looks different | Re-attach the master portrait as an Ingredient; regenerate |
| Skin looks plastic or too smooth | Add at the start: *"Unretouched skin, visible pores, natural redness and wrinkles."* |
| Too much action, clip feels rushed | Remove the last action from the prompt, then use **Extend** to add it |
| The door opens or closes wrongly | Add: *"The door is closed at the start and closes fully at the end."* |
| The creature looks cartoonish | Add: *"Real wildlife documentary footage, real animal anatomy."* |
| Wrong voice or accent | Add the voice description from the Stage 1 voice table after the dialogue |
