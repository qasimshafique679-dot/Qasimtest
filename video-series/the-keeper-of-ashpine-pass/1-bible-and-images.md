## 1.1 Source video analysis (structure only — nothing copied)

| Element | What the source does |
|---|---|
| **Core concept** | Greedy, status-hungry people celebrate a big money opportunity while underestimating someone whose true power is hidden ("hidden fortune"). |
| **Hook** | Opens mid-conversation with a big status reveal ("the right-hand man of the chairman"). |
| **Structure** | Reveal → shocked reaction → greedy excitement → plans to climb higher → cut before the hidden truth lands. |
| **Emotional arc** | Surprise → greed → smug confidence. The viewer senses they're about to be humbled. |
| **Conflict** | Ambition and greed vs. a quiet person who is more than they seem. |
| **Retention trick** | Big money number ("$30 million"), exaggerated reaction close-ups, a cut every 2–3 s, "Full episodes in bio". |
| **Ending style** | Cliffhanger on a confident face just before the fall. |

**What we keep:** greedy couple + a big money prize + an underestimated quiet person + a reaction-close-up cliffhanger.
**What we change:** everything else — a new genre (creature fantasy), a new setting (1974 snowy mountains), new characters and names, new dialogue, a creature, and a twist where the greedy man is the one caught.

## 1.2 Original story concept

- **Title:** The Keeper of Ashpine Pass
- **Genre:** Fantasy / creature thriller
- **Logline:** In a snowbound 1974 mountain town, a greedy lodge owner tracks the quiet mail carrier to collect a bounty on a legendary white beast — and learns she isn't the one who needs protecting.
- **Theme:** Greed blinds people to what is really powerful; quiet kindness earns loyalty.
- **Setting:** Ashpine Pass, a tiny logging town high in snowy pine mountains, winter 1974.

**Story in 5 acts (8 clips, 64 s)**
1. **Hook (clip 1):** In a smoky lodge bar, Walt Kessler slams a heavy leather money pouch on the counter: $5,000 to whoever finds the white beast.
2. **The quiet one (clips 2–3):** Nora, the mail carrier, comes in from the blizzard. Doreen mocks her. Walt spots a long silver-white hair on Nora's sleeve — far too big for any wolf.
3. **Greed (clip 4):** Doreen pushes Walt to follow her. He takes a coil of rope and a heavy capture net and steps into the snow.
4. **The secret (clips 5–6):** At dusk Nora skis to a hidden clearing and whistles. A horse-sized white lynx-like creature, Moss, steps out of the pines and gently lowers its head to her hand.
5. **The turn & cliffhanger (clips 7–8):** Walt readies his net, grinning. A low growl behind him. He turns — an enormous older creature, Moss's mother, stands over him. Nora: "I wouldn't move, Mr. Kessler." Cut to black on his terrified face.

## 1.3 Character Bible

### Nora Lind — the quiet keeper
- **Age / gender / background:** 28, woman, Norwegian-American
- **Face:** soft oval face, fair skin with freckles across nose and cheeks, wind-reddened cheeks, grey-green eyes, small scar through her left eyebrow
- **Hair:** light-brown, long, in one thick side braid, loose strands around her face
- **Build:** slim, athletic, about 5'7"
- **Outfit:** mustard-yellow 1970s quilted down parka, grey chunky-knit wool scarf, dark-green knit beanie, brown corduroy trousers, tan lace-up winter boots, brown leather mail satchel across her body, red wool mittens
- **Props:** wooden cross-country skis and poles
- **Personality:** calm, gentle, says little, never rushes; eyes always watchful
- **Body language:** steady posture, small careful movements, looks people straight in the eye

> **LOCKED LINE:** Nora, a 28-year-old Norwegian-American woman with a freckled oval face, wind-reddened cheeks, grey-green eyes, a small scar through her left eyebrow, a long light-brown side braid, wearing a mustard-yellow 1970s down parka, grey knit scarf, dark-green beanie and brown leather mail satchel.

### Walt Kessler — the greedy lodge owner
- **Age / gender / background:** 56, man, American
- **Face:** broad, ruddy weathered face, heavy jowls, deep forehead lines, small pale-blue eyes, thick grey mutton-chop sideburns, clean-shaven chin and upper lip (NO beard, NO moustache), slightly crooked nose
- **Hair:** thinning grey hair combed back
- **Build:** tall, heavy-set, big belly
- **Outfit:** red-and-black buffalo-plaid wool jacket over a cream thermal henley, tan corduroy trousers, wide brown leather belt with a brass buckle, worn brown leather work boots
- **Props:** a heavy closed brown leather money pouch; a coil of thick hemp rope and a folded heavy rope capture net carried over his shoulder (no guns anywhere in the story)
- **Personality:** loud, boastful, greedy, a bully who is secretly a coward
- **Body language:** chest out, takes up space, laughs too loud

> **LOCKED LINE:** Walt, a 56-year-old heavy-set American man with a ruddy weathered face, heavy jowls, small pale-blue eyes, thick grey mutton-chop sideburns, a clean-shaven chin and upper lip with no beard or moustache, thinning combed-back grey hair, wearing a red-and-black buffalo-plaid wool jacket, cream thermal shirt and tan corduroy trousers.

### Doreen Kessler — Walt's wife
- **Age / gender / background:** 53, woman, American
- **Face:** narrow face, pale skin, sharp cheekbones, thin pencilled eyebrows, hazel eyes, coral-red lipstick, fine lines around mouth
- **Hair:** auburn, teased 1970s bouffant, shoulder length
- **Build:** thin, upright
- **Outfit:** burgundy turtleneck sweater, cream cable-knit cardigan, gold cat-eye glasses on a beaded chain, small gold earrings, grey wool skirt
- **Personality:** snobbish, scheming, the real driver of Walt's greed
- **Body language:** arms crossed, chin up, looks down her glasses at people

> **LOCKED LINE:** Doreen, a 53-year-old thin American woman with a narrow pale face, sharp cheekbones, hazel eyes, coral-red lipstick, a teased auburn 1970s bouffant, wearing a burgundy turtleneck, cream cable-knit cardigan and gold cat-eye glasses on a beaded chain.

### Moss — the young creature
- **Species (original):** an "Ashpine lynx" — a giant mountain feline found only in this story
- **Size:** as big as a horse, about 5 ft at the shoulder
- **Look:** thick snow-white fur with faint silver-grey ripples along the back, long tufted ears with black tips, a short thick tail with a black tip, a fluffy ruff around the neck with tiny frost crystals in it, wide furry paws, warm amber eyes, pale pink nose
- **Personality:** gentle, curious, protective of Nora; moves slowly and softly

> **LOCKED LINE:** Moss, a horse-sized snow-white lynx-like creature with thick fur rippled with faint silver-grey, long black-tipped tufted ears, a frost-crystal neck ruff, wide furry paws, warm amber eyes and a pale pink nose.

### The Mother — the guardian
- **Same species as Moss**, so the same face shape, ears, ruff and eye colour
- **Size:** about twice Moss's size, around 10 ft at the shoulder
- **Differences:** older; darker silver-grey muzzle; a long pale scar across the bridge of the nose; heavier frost crystals in the ruff; deep low growl

> **LOCKED LINE:** the Mother, an enormous elephant-sized snow-white lynx-like creature with thick silver-rippled fur, long black-tipped tufted ears, a heavy frost-crystal ruff, a darker silver muzzle, a long pale scar across her nose and glowing amber eyes.

### Background extras (clip 1 only)
> **LOCKED LINE:** two bearded lumberjacks in their 40s wearing wool flat caps, faded denim jackets lined with sheepskin and thick work gloves.
