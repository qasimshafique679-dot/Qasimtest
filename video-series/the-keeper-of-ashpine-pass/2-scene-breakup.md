## Clip-by-clip detail

### CLIP 1 — The Bounty (0:00–0:08)
**Ingredients:** walt-master, doreen-master, lodge-interior

**START FRAME:** Wide shot of the lodge bar. Fire burning on the left. Walt stands behind the long wooden bar counter, centre-right. The two lumberjacks sit on bar stools facing him, backs half to camera. Doreen stands at the far right end of the bar, arms crossed. The heavy front door at the back of the room is closed. Snow falls past the frosted windows.

**ACTION:**
1. Walt pulls a thick stack of hundred-dollar bills tied with brown string from inside his plaid jacket.
2. He slaps it down hard on the counter.
3. He leans forward on both fists and announces his line, grinning.
4. The lumberjacks lean back and laugh, then raise their beer glasses.
5. Doreen gives a small, satisfied smile.
6. At the back of the room the front door swings open and a gust of snow blows in.

**DIALOGUE:** Walt: *"Five thousand dollars to the man who brings me that white beast."*

**END FRAME:** Walt behind the bar, both fists on the counter, the cash bundle in front of him. Lumberjacks on their stools, glasses raised. Doreen at the right end of the bar, now looking toward the door. **The front door is open**, with snow blowing in and a figure in a mustard-yellow parka standing in the doorway. Camera: wide, from behind the lumberjacks' shoulders.

### CLIP 2 — The Mail Girl (0:08–0:16)
**Ingredients:** nora-master, doreen-master, walt-master, lodge-interior

**START FRAME:** Same as the end of clip 1. Nora stands in the open doorway, snow on her shoulders and beanie. Everyone else is in the same place.

**ACTION:**
1. Nora steps fully inside.
2. She turns and pushes the door closed behind her; the iron latch clicks.
3. She stamps snow off her boots on the mat.
4. She walks to the bar and stops next to the cash bundle, beside Walt.
5. She takes a small stack of envelopes out of her leather satchel and lays it on the counter.
6. Doreen looks her up and down over her cat-eye glasses and says her line.
7. Nora answers quietly, without looking up.
8. Nora rests her right forearm on the counter.

**DIALOGUE:** Doreen: *"You're dripping on my floor, mail girl."* · Nora (quietly): *"Sorry, Mrs. Kessler."*

**END FRAME:** **Door closed.** Nora at the bar, her right forearm on the counter, the envelopes in front of her. **One long silver-white hair is caught on her right parka sleeve**, catching the lamp light. Walt behind the bar, eyes moving down to the sleeve. Doreen on the right, arms crossed. Lumberjacks in the background. Camera: medium shot of Nora and Walt across the bar.

### CLIP 3 — The White Hair (0:16–0:24)
**Ingredients:** walt-master, nora-master, lodge-interior

**START FRAME:** Same as the end of clip 2. The hair is on Nora's right sleeve; Walt is looking at it.

**ACTION:**
1. Close-up: Walt's thick fingers pinch the long silver-white hair and lift it off Nora's sleeve.
2. He holds it up to the orange lamp; it shimmers, about a foot long.
3. His eyes narrow; he looks from the hair to Nora and says his line.
4. Nora meets his eyes calmly and answers.
5. She turns, walks to the front door, lifts the latch, opens the door, steps outside, and pulls it closed behind her.
6. Walt closes his fist around the hair, staring at the door.

**DIALOGUE:** Walt: *"That's no wolf. Where've you been, Nora?"* · Nora: *"Delivering mail."*

**END FRAME:** **Front door closed, Nora outside (not visible).** Walt behind the bar, the hair in his closed right fist, staring at the door. Doreen at the right end of the bar, now staring at Walt's fist. Envelopes and cash still on the counter. Camera: medium close-up on Walt.

---

### CLIP 4 — The Hunt Begins (0:24–0:32)
**Ingredients:** doreen-master, walt-master, lodge-interior, lodge-exterior

**START FRAME:** Same as the end of clip 3.

**ACTION:**
1. Doreen walks over and leans close to Walt's ear, whispering her line.
2. Walt nods slowly, a greedy smile spreading.
3. He walks to the wall rack beside the fireplace and lifts down the wooden-stock hunting rifle; he slings it over his right shoulder.
4. He takes a brown leather cap with ear flaps and brown leather gloves from a hook and puts them on.
5. He walks to the front door, opens it, and steps out onto the snowy porch; the camera follows him through the doorway.
6. The door swings closed behind him.
7. Outside, he looks down at the snow.

**DIALOGUE:** Doreen (whispering): *"Follow her, Walt. Five thousand dollars."*

**CHANGE:** Walt now wears the leather cap and gloves and carries the slung rifle (kept from now on).

**END FRAME:** **Exterior.** Walt standing on the snowy porch steps of the lodge, the front door closed behind him with warm light at the windows. Rifle slung on his right shoulder, cap and gloves on. He is looking at **a fresh line of ski tracks** leading down Main Street toward the dark pines. The snow-covered pickup truck is on the left. Blue dusk, gentle snowfall. Camera: medium-wide from behind Walt's left shoulder.

### CLIP 5 — The Tracks (0:32–0:40)
**Ingredients:** walt-master, nora-master, lodge-exterior, clearing

**START FRAME:** Same as the end of clip 4.

**ACTION:**
1. Walt steps down off the porch and follows the ski tracks along the street, past the pickup truck.
2. Tracking shot: he walks into the pines, breathing hard, his breath fogging.
3. He reaches the edge of the clearing and crouches behind a large snow-dusted grey boulder.
4. He peers over the top.
5. In the clearing, about 30 metres away, Nora unclips her skis and leans them and her poles against the fallen pine log.

**DIALOGUE:** None. Sound: boots crunching in snow, heavy breathing, wind in the pines.

**END FRAME:** Walt crouched behind the grey boulder at the left edge of the clearing, only his head and cap above it, rifle still slung. Nora standing in the centre of the clearing in the deep snow, facing the far tree line, her skis leaning on the fallen log behind her. Frozen creek on the right. Camera: over Walt's shoulder, Nora small in the distance.

---

### CLIP 6 — Moss (0:40–0:48)
**Ingredients:** nora-master, moss-master, clearing

**START FRAME:** Same as the end of clip 5, seen from the clearing: Nora in the centre facing the far tree line, skis on the log. (Walt hidden behind the boulder, out of focus in the far background.)

**ACTION:**
1. Nora pulls off one red mitten and gives two soft whistles.
2. Silence. Snow falls.
3. The snow-loaded branches at the far tree line shift; snow slides off them.
4. Moss steps slowly out of the pines and walks through the deep snow toward her, ears forward.
5. He stops in front of her and gently lowers his huge head.
6. Nora places her bare hand on his muzzle, and he gives a soft chuff.
7. She glances back toward the boulder and whispers her line.

**DIALOGUE:** Nora (whispering): *"Easy, Moss. Somebody followed me."*

**END FRAME:** Centre of the clearing. Nora standing with her bare right hand on Moss's muzzle, head turned toward the boulder at the left. Moss with his head lowered and his amber eyes looking toward the same boulder. In the far left background, Walt rising slightly behind the boulder. Camera: medium two-shot of Nora and Moss in profile.

---

### CLIP 7 — The Growl (0:48–0:56)
**Ingredients:** walt-master, moss-master, mother-master, clearing

**START FRAME:** Behind the boulder: Walt crouched, Nora and Moss in the centre of the clearing in the distance, as at the end of clip 6.

**ACTION:**
1. Walt slides the rifle off his shoulder and rests it on top of the boulder, the barrel pointing at the ground in front of the creature. He does not fire.
2. Close-up: his greedy, sweating face; he whispers his line.
3. A low, deep growl rumbles **behind** him.
4. A cloud of warm breath fogs over his right shoulder, and snowflakes swirl in it.
5. His grin fades. His eyes go wide. He stops breathing.

**DIALOGUE:** Walt (whispering): *"Five thousand dollars… right there."*

**END FRAME:** Walt frozen behind the boulder, his gloved hands still on the rifle resting on the rock, eyes wide, facing the clearing. Fog from warm breath hangs over his right shoulder. A huge **shadow** falls across him and the boulder from behind. Moonlight is starting to break through the clouds. Camera: close-up of Walt's face, low angle.

---

### CLIP 8 — The Mother (0:56–1:04)
**Ingredients:** walt-master, mother-master, nora-master, clearing

**START FRAME:** Same as the end of clip 7.

**ACTION:**
1. Walt slowly turns his head, then his body, looking up.
2. Low-angle reveal: the Mother stands over him, enormous, her scarred face just a metre above his, her amber eyes glowing in the moonlight.
3. Nora's calm voice carries from the clearing (Nora out of focus in the far background, Moss beside her).
4. Walt's gloved hands open; the rifle slides off the boulder and drops into the deep snow.
5. The Mother lowers her face closer and exhales; her frosty breath washes over him.
6. Extreme close-up of Walt's terrified face. Hard cut to black.

**DIALOGUE:** Nora (calm, from a distance): *"I wouldn't move, Mr. Kessler. She doesn't like guns."*

**END FRAME (final):** Extreme close-up of Walt's terrified face lit by cold moonlight, frost on his sideburns, the Mother's breath fogging across the frame. The rifle lies in the snow at his feet. Cut to black. *(Cliffhanger for episode 2.)*
