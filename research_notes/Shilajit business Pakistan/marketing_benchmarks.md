_Research date: 7 Nov 2026. Method: web search only. WebFetch was blocked for transparency.meta.com and siffar.com (outside this session's allowed network hosts), so all figures come from search-result summaries, not full-page reads. ## 1. Meta and TikTok ad policies for supplements / sexual wellness / health claims

### Takeaway
Shilajit can be advertised on Meta and TikTok as a general "energy / vitality / wellness" supplement. Ads get rejected when they make sexual-performance, ED, libido, "testosterone booster", disease-cure or weight-loss claims. In Pakistan, DRAP rules also require ad claims to match the claims approved at product enlistment, and they require pre-clearance from the DRAP Advertisement Board.

### Cited Findings
**Meta (Facebook/Instagram)**
- Meta's Health & Wellness ad policy covers two categories: (a) Weight Loss and Cosmetic Products and Procedures, and (b) Adult Products and Reproductive Health. — [Meta Transparency Center: Health and Wellness](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/health-wellness)
- Meta does not allow ads for dietary weight-loss products such as weight-loss pills or supplements. — [Meta Transparency Center](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/health-wellness) (search snippet; the full page could not be fetched)
- Sexual and reproductive wellness ads are allowed if they focus on health. They must target users 18+ and must not focus on sexual pleasure. — [Meta Transparency Center](https://transparency.meta.com/en-gb/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/health-wellness)
- A third-party guide says 18+ age targeting applies to weight-loss products, cosmetic procedures and dietary supplements. — [Madgicx: Facebook ad guidelines 2025](https://madgicx.com/blog/facebook-ad-guidelines)
- Third-party summaries say disease-cure claims and sexual-enhancement products are banned in every category. — [Accelerated Digital Media: 2026 Health Advertising Policies](https://www.accelerateddigitalmedia.com/insights/guide-to-social-media-health-ad-restrictions-2026/); [OuterBox](https://www.outerboxdesign.com/articles/digital-marketing/how-health-and-wellness-brands-can-keep-their-ads-compliant-on-meta-and-other-social-media-platforms/)
- A Meta spokesperson said: "We welcome ads for sexual wellness products but we prohibit nudity and have specific rules about how these products can be marketed." A Center for Intimacy Justice report alleges that Meta rejects women's-health ads while approving men's sexual-wellness ads. This is an allegation, not a Meta finding. — [Marketing-Interactive](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/facebook-and-instagram-accused-of-rejecting-women-s-health-ads)

**TikTok**
- TikTok does not accept ads for weight-loss or weight-management supplements, including fat burners, appetite suppressants, and detox teas or lollipops. — [TikTok Ad Policy Handbook: North America](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/ad-policy-handbook-north-america?lang=en)
- TikTok's Healthcare & Pharmaceuticals policy: sexual-health products such as condoms and lubricant must target 18+ and must not "focus on sexual pleasure or be overtly sexual in nature". — [TikTok Ads Policy: Healthcare and Pharmaceuticals](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-healthcare-pharmaceuticals)
- TikTok has a separate handbook for the Middle East, Turkey, Africa and Pakistan (METAP) region. Pakistan campaigns fall under it, so check it for country-specific rules. — [TikTok Ad Policy Handbook: METAP](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/ad-policy-handbook-metap?lang=en); [Ecomdy: METAP TikTok policies](https://ecomdymedia.com/blog/metap-tiktok-ads-key-policies-every-advertiser-must-know)
- TikTok has run age restrictions on weight-related ads since 2020, limiting them to 18+. — [Tubefilter (2020)](https://tubefilter.com/2020/09/23/tiktok-diet-pill-body-positivity-ad-policies/)

**Pakistan regulator (DRAP)**
- Nutraceutical and health-product enlistment falls under the Alternative Medicines and Health Products (Enlistment) Rules 2014 (SRO 412(I)/2014), made under the DRAP Act 2012. — [Sindh High Court order](https://caselaw.shc.gov.pk/caselaw/view-file/MTU4NjI0Y2Ztcy1kYzgz); [DRAP enlistment applications](https://www.dra.gov.pk/?p=34931)
- DRAP's January 2025 advertisement rules require ad claims to match the claims approved at registration or enlistment. — [DRAP Advertisement rules (PDF, Jan 2025)](https://dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Advertisement-rules.pdf); [Profit/Pakistan Today, 1 Feb 2025: "Govt bans advertisement of medical products without prior approval"](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2025/02/01/govt-bans-advertisement-of-medical-products-without-prior-approval)
- In February 2026 DRAP warned companies that any registered or listed therapeutic product needs DRAP Advertisement Board clearance before it is advertised. It also flagged "complete cure / guaranteed treatment" claims. — [ProPakistani, 12 Feb 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/02/12/drap-warns-companies-over-unapproved-medicine-advertisements/amp/)
- More than 30,000 enlistment applications are reportedly pending with DRAP's Health & OTC directorate. — [ProPakistani, 24 Sep 2025](https://propakistani.pk/2025/09/24/tip-slams-drap-for-failing-to-regulate-alternative-medicines-despite-court-orders/amp/)

**How shilajit is marketed / claim-risk wording**
- Influencer content frames shilajit as a "destroyer of weakness". Western male influencers push testosterone and libido claims. — [The Juggernaut](https://www.thejuggernaut.com/shilajit-benefits-science-hype-ayurveda); [NewsSniffer/BBC-style piece](https://newssniffer.co.uk/articles/2775787/versions/16050932)
- The testosterone claim rests mainly on one small 2016 *Andrologia* trial (about 20% rise in total testosterone). Independent reviews call the evidence insufficient. — [WellnessPulse](https://wellnesspulse.com/nutrition/shilajit-for-testosterone-scientific-evidence/); [MDLinx](https://www.mdlinx.com/article/your-patients-are-using-shilajit-to-enhance-mental-clarity-and-improve-their-sex-life/3aZe2gOnmDz3vA4rb3PjxH)

### Inferences
Claim-risk ladder, synthesized from the policies above:

| Risk | Example wording | Likely outcome on Meta/TikTok |
|---|---|---|
| Low | "Natural energy", "daily vitality", "Himalayan mineral resin", "fulvic acid & 80+ minerals", "lab-tested purity", "sourced from Gilgit-Baltistan" | Usually approved. Set 18+ targeting as a precaution |
| Medium | "Stamina", "strength", "endurance for gym", "men's wellness", "recovery" | Often approved, but borderline when combined with male imagery or bedroom context |
| High | "Testosterone booster", "boost T levels", "libido", "timing", "mardana taqat", "sexual power" | Frequent rejections. Can trigger personal-attribute and sexual-enhancement flags, and repeat strikes risk ad-account restriction |
| Banned | "Cures ED / diabetes / infertility", "guaranteed results", before/after body shots, weight-loss claims | Rejected. Also breaches DRAP rules against cure claims |

- Pakistani shilajit sellers commonly use Urdu and Roman-Urdu euphemisms such as "taqat" and "josh". Meta's review covers Urdu text, so euphemisms do not make a claim safe. (This is an inference. No source was found on how Meta enforces Urdu text.)
- A practical setup is a "clean" ad account and landing page using compliant wellness claims, with the riskier stamina messaging moved to organic content and WhatsApp follow-up. Even then, DRAP rules still apply to all advertising, including organic posts.

### Gaps
- Could not fetch the full Meta policy text, so the exact wording on supplements outside weight loss and on "sexual performance enhancement" is unconfirmed beyond the search snippets.
- No official TikTok METAP text was read for Pakistan-specific supplement rules.
- Unclear whether shilajit resin needs DRAP enlistment as a nutraceutical, or whether it can be sold as a food product under provincial food authority rules. Needs a regulatory check.

## 2. Pakistan ad cost benchmarks (Meta, TikTok, Google) and search demand

### Takeaway
Pakistan's Meta CPMs are among the cheapest in the world, roughly USD 0.60–2.90 (about PKR 170–800). Sources disagree widely, and no independent Pakistan benchmark exists. TikTok CPM estimates conflict (PKR 150–1,100 versus USD 4–8). Planning ROAS for COD e-commerce should be about 2–3.5x before returns, not the inflated 6–9x claims some agencies make.

## 3. Influencer marketing rates in Pakistan and influencer types

### Takeaway
Micro-influencers (5k–50k followers) cost roughly PKR 5,000–25,000 per post. Mid-tier (50k–500k) costs PKR 25,000–150,000. Macro and celebrity creators cost PKR 80,000–800,000+ per post, and big YouTubers PKR 500k–2M. All of these come from inconsistent agency blogs. Agencies advise long-term micro-influencer partnerships over one-off posts.

### Inferences
- The best-fit creator types for shilajit in Pakistan (inference) are gym and fitness coaches, bodybuilders, and natural-lifestyle or hiking/Gilgit-Baltistan travel vloggers. The travel vloggers suit authenticity and sourcing stories. Desi-dad or "husband" comedy creators suit gifting angles, and hakeem or desi-nutrition explainers suit education. Creators' paid posts are still ads under DRAP rules, so brief them on compliant claims.
- Sample monthly influencer budget (estimate): 10 micro creators × PKR 15k plus 2 mid-tier × PKR 60k comes to about PKR 270k. Add whitelisting (running creator posts as paid ads), which costs extra.

## 4. Marketplaces and other channels

### Takeaway
Daraz Health & Beauty commission is about 7–13% depending on subcategory, from undated or third-party data. All-in Daraz costs, including shipping, promotions and returns, are reported at 15–18%+ of revenue. Daraz Mall requires verified brand ownership, which in practice means trademark registration or an authorisation letter, and since November 2025 it carries an authenticity guarantee.

### Cited Findings
- Daraz category commission table (undated Scribd profit calculator): "All other Health & Beauty" 12.90%, Medical Supplies 8.60%, OTC Medicines 6.00%. — [Scribd Daraz Profit Calculator](https://www.scribd.com/document/482319994/Daraz-Profit-Calculator-xlsx)

- Total Daraz cost (commission, shipping, promotions and return handling) frequently exceeds 15–18% of gross revenue. Sponsored ads and Finance Act 2025 withholding tax come on top. — [WeProms: hidden ecommerce costs](https://weproms-pk.pages.dev/blog/ecommerce-hidden-costs-pakistan-profit-margins/); [Baco Consultants: Daraz seller tax 2026](https://bacoconsultants.com/blogs/daraz-seller-tax-pakistan-2026)

- DarazMall introduced an "authenticity guarantee" ahead of 11.11 in November 2025. Every purchase comes from verified brand partners or sellers. — [ProPakistani, 5 Nov 2025](https://propakistani.pk/2025/11/05/darazmall-introduces-authenticity-guarantee-ahead-of-11-11-to-elevate-brand-shopping-experience/amp/); [TBS News](https://www.tbsnews.net/economy/corporates/daraz-revamps-darazmall-authenticity-guarantee-1273526)

| Channel | Role for a shilajit brand | Cost notes (estimates) |
|---|---|---|
| Own Shopify/WooCommerce site + Meta/TikTok | Core D2C, highest margin, owns customer data | Ad CAC per section 2 |
| Daraz (regular) | Discovery and trust for search-led buyers. Fake-shilajit competition is heavy | ~8–13% commission + fees, ~15–18% all-in |
| Daraz Mall | Authenticity signal, which matters in a category full of fakes | Needs a registered trademark (IPO Pakistan) and probably DRAP or food-authority documents |
| WhatsApp Business / Catalog | Order confirmation (cuts RTO), repeat orders, subscriptions | Low cost; WhatsApp API is paid per conversation |
| Facebook Marketplace / groups | Low-cost local reach | Organic |
| Gyms, supplement stores, pharmacies, herbal/dry-fruit shops | Offline trust and sampling | Retail margin, likely 20–35% (estimate, unsourced) |
| International (Amazon, Etsy, own export site) | Higher price per gram. Strong demand in the US and UK | FDA and marketplace supplement rules are strict. Amazon requires testing documents (not researched here) |

### Gaps

- No data on pharmacy or gym retail margins, or Amazon/Etsy rules for shilajit (Etsy generally restricts ingestible supplements; not verified here).

## 5. Typical Pakistani D2C e-commerce metrics

### Takeaway
Pakistan e-commerce runs on COD. RTO (return-to-origin) clusters around 18–30%, so plan for about 20%. Plan conversion rate at roughly 1–2%, borrowing global Shopify medians, and CAC at roughly PKR 2,000–3,500 from limited evidence. No reliable market-wide AOV or repeat-rate data exists.

### Inferences
- A unit-economics starting point (estimate): shilajit 20g jar at PKR 2,500–4,500, AOV of about PKR 3,500–5,000 with bundles, 20% RTO, and a reverse-logistics cost per returned parcel. Effective CAC per delivered order is about PKR 2,500–4,000. Profitability depends on second orders, since shilajit is a 30–60 day consumable. That makes WhatsApp-based reorders and subscriptions important.
