## Which established brands sell shilajit in Pakistan; since when; company background?

### Takeaway
The market splits in two. (1) Old tibb/Unani pharma houses (Hamdard 1948, Ajmal Dawakhana c.1949, Qarshi, plus dawakhanas such as Al-Saudia Tibbi and Siddiqui) sell salajeet only as a cheap compound ingredient: qurs (tablets), habb (pills), akseer and majoon. None of them was found selling pure resin. (2) Pure resin is sold by Gilgit-Baltistan, Hunza and Chitral heritage and D2C brands (Hunza Foodways, Organic Aprico, Chitral House, Chiltan Pure, Taj Sultan, Original Shilajit PK) and by Hemani, a large herbal FMCG company. No shilajit SKU was found for Marhaba Laboratories, and no current one for Qarshi.

### Cited Findings
- **Hamdard Pakistan:** traces back to Hamdard Dawakhana, founded in Delhi in 1906. Hakim Mohammed Said set up the Pakistani company as Hamdard Laboratories (Waqf) in 1948 — [Wikipedia: Hamdard Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamdard_Pakistan); [Wikipedia: Hamdard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamdard)
- Sehat.com.pk names the vendor of Habb-e-Amber Momyaie as "Hamdard Laboratories (Waqf), Pakistan" — [Sehat via search](https://sehat.com.pk/products/Qurs-Salajeet-20qurs.html)
- **Dawakhana Hakim Ajmal Khan (Ajmal):** according to LinkedIn, Hakeem Ajmal Khan migrated to Lahore in 1948 and founded "Ajmal Dawakhana" a year later — [LinkedIn DHAK](https://pk.linkedin.com/company/dhak). Wikipedia instead credits Hakim Nabi with opening Dawakhana Hakim Ajmal Khan in Lahore (a conflict between sources) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hakim_Ajmal_Khan)
- **Qarshi:** named after Hakim Muhammad Hassan Qarshi, a Unani practitioner and founder of Qarshi Dawakhana — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hakim_Muhammad_Hassan_Qarshi). A seller blog says Qarshi's "Shilajit is widely available", but I found no Qarshi salajeet product page or price — [AjyalFoods blog](https://www.ajyalfoods.com/blogs/news/top-15-best-pure-salajeet-brands-in-pakistan-2025)
- **Marhaba Laboratories (Lahore, Quaid-e-Azam Industrial Estate):** makes herbal medicines, dietary supplements and personal care products (syrups, Marhaba Honey, Shape Up, balm, mouthwash, inhaler). No salajeet product was found — [Sehat manufacturer page](https://sehat.com.pk/manufacturers/Marhaba-Laboratories-PVT-LTD.html); [Marhaba shop](https://marhabahealthcareshop.com/collections/health-care); [Panjiva](https://panjiva.com/Marhaba-Laboratories-Pvt-Ltd/15449941)
- **Hemani (herbal FMCG):** sells "Pure Shilajit 50g" resin in Pakistan and also exports it (sold in the US, Finland and on eBay) — [Taj Sultan price guide](https://tajsultan.com/blogs/news/shilajit-price-in-pakistan); [theshoptank](https://theshoptank.com/products/hemani-pure-shilajit-50g); [indianmarket.fi](https://indianmarket.fi/products/hemani-pure-shilajit-50g)

- **Organic Aprico (Gilgit-Baltistan resin):** 4.8/5 from 40 Google reviews. Its own page warns that much of the shilajit in the Pakistani market is counterfeit — [Organic Aprico](https://organicaprico.com/products/shilajit); [organicaprico.com](https://organicaprico.com/?p=2879)
- **Chitral House:** sells resin, gummies and capsules and publishes a lab report, according to a competitor's ranking — [Chitral House gummies](https://chitralhouse.com/collections/shilajit-gummies); [Taj Sultan guide](https://medium.com/@tajsultanpvt/shilajit-price-in-pakistan-2026-10g-to-1kg-price-per-gram-3190de4be509)
- **Chiltan Pure:** sells resin and shilajit honey. It also has a wholesale site and is resold on Mamasjan — [Chiltan Pure](https://chiltanpure.com/products/shilajit); [wholesale](https://wholesale.chiltanpure.com/products/chiltanpure-shilajit); [Mamasjan](https://mamasjan.com/products/chiltanpure-shilajit?variant=43700444594449)
- **Original Shilajit PK:** cites a Eurofins lab report (arsenic 11.98 mg/kg) — [Taj Sultan guide](https://medium.com/@tajsultanpvt/shilajit-price-in-pakistan-2026-10g-to-1kg-price-per-gram-3190de4be509)
- **Taj Sultan:** a newer D2C brand that markets "PCSIR-tested" salajeet and publishes "top brands" rankings in which it ranks itself first (self-disclosed) — [Taj Sultan collection](https://tajsultan.com/collections/shilajit-in-pakistan); [Top 3 ranking](https://medium.com/@tajsultanpvt/top-3-shilajit-brands-in-pakistan-2026-ranked-by-their-lab-reports-558110c292f2)
- **Al-Saudia Tibbi Foundation** and **Siddiqui Dawakhana:** tibb sellers that list salajeet majoon and capsules — [Al-Saudia](https://alsaudiatibbifoundation.com.pk/products/majoon-salajeet); [Siddiqui Dawakhana](https://siddiquidawakhana.org/product/salajeet/)

### Inferences
- The "old" brands (Hamdard, Ajmal, Qarshi) are old as companies, but their shilajit is a low-dose ingredient in Rs 50–750 compound medicines. None of them competes in pure resin, so that segment has no legacy pharma leader.
- In Unani usage "Momyaie/Mumiyai" is commonly treated as a synonym for shilajit/mumijo. This would make Hamdard's and Qarshi's Habb-e-Amber Momyaie shilajit-containing products, but no source confirmed the ingredient list.
- Most "brand comparison" content in Pakistan is written by sellers (Taj Sultan, Organic Aprico, Chitral House blogs), so rankings are biased.

### Gaps
- Founding years for Hunza Foodways, Organic Aprico, Chitral House, Chiltan Pure and Original Shilajit PK were not found.
- I could not confirm whether Qarshi currently sells a salajeet SKU, because qarshi.com and qarshihealthshop.com could not be fetched.
- I could not find whether Dr. Khan Dawakhana or other well-known dawakhanas sell a salajeet SKU.
- No large nutraceutical brand (Nutrifactor, Sunny Health, Getz, etc.) was found with a shilajit SKU in the search results.

## Product forms, pack sizes and current PKR prices (SKU level, price per gram)

### Takeaway
Pure resin from GB and Hunza brands retails at about Rs 118–150/g in 10–25g jars and Rs 85–95/g per kg wholesale. Hemani is the outlier at Rs 34/g. Gummies and capsules cost about Rs 2,000–3,000 per pack. Tibb compound products are far cheaper (Rs 50–750), except premium majoons such as Al-Saudia's Salajeet Kasturi at Rs 11,000.

### Inferences
- **Price-per-gram ladder (resin):** Hemani about Rs 34 < Chitral House 70g about Rs 64 < bulk 1kg Rs 85–95 < Original Shilajit PK 60g Rs 92 < 20–25g jars Rs 118–145 < 10g jars Rs 126–150. Small jars carry a premium of about 40–75% over kilo wholesale.
- Hunza Foodways and Organic Aprico each raised prices modestly (about Rs 30–200 per SKU) between page versions, while Hunza's 25g price dipped slightly.
- Gummies cost about 2–3x more per gram of actual shilajit than resin, which points to a margin-rich format.
- Common SKU sizes are 10g, 20g, 25g, 50g, 60g and 1kg for resin; 40 or 60 count for gummies and capsules.

## Claims, packaging and channels

### Takeaway
Lab reports have become the main competitive claim: Eurofins (Original Shilajit PK), PCSIR (Taj Sultan) and Chitral House's published reports. Hemani publishes none. I found no DRAP registration number for any resin brand in the sources I could reach. Tibb products sell through pharmacies, superstores and own web shops; resin brands sell D2C through Shopify sites with COD, plus Daraz and resellers.

### Cited Findings
- Taj Sultan scores brands on a 12-point scale that rewards published lab reports and ranks itself first by its own disclosure. Original Shilajit PK is second, with a Eurofins report (arsenic 11.98 mg/kg) — [Taj Sultan Medium ranking](https://medium.com/@tajsultanpvt/top-3-shilajit-brands-in-pakistan-2026-ranked-by-their-lab-reports-558110c292f2); [Taj Sultan guide](https://medium.com/@tajsultanpvt/shilajit-price-in-pakistan-2026-10g-to-1kg-price-per-gram-3190de4be509)

- Hemani's 50g pack has no published lab report. Its export listings claim "Natural, Halal, Non-GMO" — [Taj Sultan guide](https://tajsultan.com/blogs/news/shilajit-price-in-pakistan); [theshoptank](https://theshoptank.com/products/hemani-pure-shilajit-50g); [eBay](https://www.ebay.com/itm/176088625977)

### Inferences
- With no DRAP registration visible for resin brands, claims compete on third-party lab names (Eurofins, PCSIR) rather than regulatory status. A new entrant showing a DRAP or PCSIR number alongside a lab COA would stand out.
- Packaging (glass vs. plastic jar) could not be verified for any brand.

### Gaps
- Fulvic acid % claims by brand were not captured.
- DRAP registration numbers were not visible in any accessible source.
- Packaging type (glass/plastic jar, blister) was not captured; Hamdard qurs are presumably strips or bottles, but this is unverified.
