## 4. Offer benchmarks (PKR)

- **Free-delivery threshold:** none (Taj Sultan, Baltistan Salajeets); Rs 2,000 (Chitrali, Jacked); Rs 3,000 (Chitral House); Rs 4,000 (Chitral Herbs).
- **COD:** universal and table stakes.
- **Discount mechanics:** strike-through 14-50%; buy 60g get 10g free; 3-for-2; seasonal sales (Pre-Winter); card-payment discount (Jacked) to cut COD.
- **Guarantee ladder:** 15-day (Original PK), 30-day (Chitral House), 60-day no-return refund (Taj Sultan, the strongest).
- **Price per gram:** ~Rs 70 (Chitral House 10g) to Rs 120 (Taj Sultan 20g) to Rs 130 (Original PK) to Rs 300 (Jacked 8g).

## 5. Compliance constraints affecting ads

- **Meta:** sexual-enhancement products and copy are restricted or banned; detailed health-interest targeting is limited; testimonials claiming cures still violate policy; ads target 18+. Keep copy to "energy, vitality, wellness". Sources: stackmatix.com/blog/meta-ads-policy, adligator.com/blog/meta-health-wellness-ad-policy-update-2026, leadenforce.com/blog/creating-facebook-ads-that-sell-supplements-without-triggering-rejection
- **DRAP:** the Therapeutic Goods (Advertisement) Rules 2025 are in force. Pre-clearance by the DRAP Advertisement Board is required for registered/enlisted products, nutraceutical companies are explicitly covered, and violations are referred to FIA (Feb 2026). Sources: propakistani.pk/2026/02/12/drap-warns-companies-over-unapproved-medicine-advertisements/amp/, dra.gov.pk/?p=1614, dailyausaf.com/en/health/drap-cracks-down-on-unapproved-medicine-advertisements/
