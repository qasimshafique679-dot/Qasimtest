# Legal, Regulatory and Lab-Testing Requirements to Sell Shilajit (Salajeet) Online in Pakistan

Research date: 7 Nov 2026. Method: web search only. **Important limitation:** direct page fetches of dra.gov.pk failed in this session (host blocked by the sandbox network), so DRAP primary documents (SRO 412(I)/2014, the fee PDFs, the HOTC guidelines, the rapid alerts) could only be read through search-engine snippets. Every DRAP fee, form number and timeline below should be checked against the original PDF, or with a DRAP-registered regulatory consultant, before you rely on it.

## 1. Which authority regulates shilajit, and how is it classified?

### Takeaway
Packaged shilajit sold for consumption with health or "strength" positioning most likely falls under DRAP's Health & OTC (non-drugs) Division. It would be classed as a "health product" (nutraceutical, food or dietary supplement), or as an "alternative medicine" (Unani/Ayurvedic/traditional) if it is presented as a traditional remedy. Under the Alternative Medicines and Health Products (Enlistment) Rules 2014, the product and its manufacturer or importer must be enlisted before it can be sold legally, and selling online is no exception. Provincial food authorities and PSQCA play at most a secondary role. I found no official ruling that classifies shilajit specifically.

### Cited Findings
- The Alternative Medicines and Health Products (Enlistment) Rules 2014 were notified as SRO 412(I)/2014 on 29-05-2014 under section 23 of the DRAP Act 2012. — [PCDA summary](http://www.pcdapakistan.org/draft-for-approval-sro-medicines-and-health-products-enlistment-rules-2013/); [FAOLEX](https://www.fao.org/faolex/results/details/en/c/LEX-FAOC189938/); [DRAP SRO PDF](https://www.dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/SRO412-I-2014-Alternative-Medicines.pdf)
- DRAP's Division of Health & OTC (non-drugs) enlists manufacturers and importers of alternative medicines and health products. — [DRAP HOTC guidelines](https://www.dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/HOTC-guidelines.pdf) (snippet)
- The rules cover food supplements ("nutraceuticals or dietary or health supplements"). DRAP's 2014 public notice also lists nutritional products and food supplements. — [PCDA/DRAP public notice](http://www.pcdapakistan.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/DRAP-PUBLIC-NOTICE-on-Alternative-Medicine-and-Health-Care-Products.pdf); [Business Recorder 2014](https://fp.brecorder.com/2014/06/201406171193424/)
- A recent DRAP clarification on SRO 1688 says supplements such as probiotics remain "Health and OTC products" (non-drugs) with an *enlistment* number, which is different from a drug *registration* number. — [Pakera](https://pakera.pk/drap-clarifies-sro-1688-probiotics-supplements-no-new-category/)
- Under the rules, all such products must be enlisted before they can be legally sold. Importers need Form 6 (firm enlistment) and Form 7 for each product. — [TIPP (Trade Information Portal of Pakistan)](https://tipp.gov.pk/index.php?r=searchProcedure/view1&id=60) (snippet)
- Customs will not release consignments of goods that need DRAP enlistment unless both the importer and the product are enlisted. — [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/4651183/customs-to-release-health-products-as-per-drap-enlistment-20181124426039)
- Eligible applicants include manufacturers with manufacturing and QC facilities, **contract givers under rule 6**, and importers authorised by the foreign manufacturer. Contract-manufacturing enlistment certificates are issued on Form 8 (Schedule A). — [DRAP SRO 412(I)/2014](https://www.dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/SRO412-I-2014-Alternative-Medicines.pdf) (snippet)
- In 2016, manufacturing alternative medicines without enlistment was barred after 1 Oct (DRAP deadline). — [Geo News](https://www.geo.tv/latest/75258-no-manufacturing-of-alternative-medicines-after-oct-1)
- A 2021 review treats shilajit as a Pakistani traditional medicine / "health product". It flags risk from consuming native (unpurified) shilajit because of its high free-radical content and possible mycotoxin-producing fungi. — [ResearchGate review](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/356505030_Review_on_Shilajit_Used_as_Pakistani_Traditional_Medicine)
- I found no DRAP enlistment record for any shilajit brand. One Pakistani seller (Ibex Salajeet) claims batch testing by Eurofins, Bureau Veritas and "DRAP", but that is a testing claim, not a published enlistment number. — [ibexsalajeet.com](https://ibexsalajeet.com/); [Jacked Nutrition listing](https://jackednutrition.pk/products/jacked-shilajit) (no DRAP number shown)

### Inferences
- **Classification:** purified shilajit resin, powder or capsules sold as a supplement best fits "health product" (nutraceutical). If it is labelled as a Unani/Ayurvedic remedy (for example "kushta" or traditional formulations), it shifts to "alternative medicine", which carries its own enlistment path and stricter claims scrutiny. Making a disease or sexual-dysfunction claim could push it towards being treated as a drug, which needs full registration under the Drugs Act 1976 / DRAP Act 2012. That route is not realistic for a startup.
- **Online sales:** the rules regulate sale, not the sales channel. The DRAP alerts below explicitly target "online sellers and social media", so selling online without enlistment carries the same risk as selling in a shop.
- **Contract manufacturer route (practical for a startup):** have an already DRAP-enlisted manufacturer (with a Health & OTC establishment licence) produce and pack the product. Then either (a) enlist the product in the manufacturer's name and sell it as distributor or marketer, or (b) enlist your own firm as a "contract giver" under rule 6 (Form 8 certificate) and enlist the product in your brand name. Confirm the current form set with a consultant.
- **Provincial food authorities (PFA, SFA):** if shilajit were positioned as a plain "food" (no health positioning), a provincial food licence would apply instead of DRAP. Shilajit is almost always marketed for health or vigour, so DRAP is the safer assumption. Where both could apply (for example a food-grade product sold from premises in Punjab), some businesses hold both. This point is **unclear** and should be confirmed with DRAP and the PFA.
- **PSQCA:** I found no evidence of a compulsory PSQCA standard for shilajit. PSQCA's mandatory list mainly covers specific foods and consumer goods.

### Gaps
- No official DRAP or PFA ruling that classifies shilajit was found (dra.gov.pk could not be fetched).
- I could not verify whether DRAP's online enlistment database (PRIMS / e-portal) lists any shilajit product.

## 2. The DRAP enlistment process: forms, fees, documents, timelines, labelling, advertising

### Takeaway
There are two steps: (1) enlist the establishment (manufacturer, importer, or contract giver), and (2) enlist each product. Search snippets of DRAP's August 2025 revised fee schedule show product-enlistment fees in the low thousands of PKR (around PKR 3,100 and 6,300) and firm or site fees of around PKR 10,400 to 19,900, but the column mapping could not be confirmed. DRAP's own target is 90 days for product enlistment (Form 7). Advertising needs prior DRAP permission, and disease or cure claims are not allowed without approval.

### Cited Findings
- **Firm enlistment forms:** Form 1 = enlistment of firm as manufacturer; Form 2 = enlistment of firm as importer (DRAP Word templates plus a Form 1 checklist). — [DRAP Form 1](https://www.dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/12.-Application-form-1-for-enlistment-of-firm-as-manufacturer.docx); [DRAP Form 2](https://www.dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/13.-Application-form-2-for-enlistment-of-firm-as-importer.docx); [Form 1 checklist](https://www.dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/3.-Checklist-for-Form-1-for-Enlistment-of-Firm-as-Manufacturer-of-Alternative-Medicines-and-Health-Products-.docx)
- **Product enlistment forms (sources conflict):** DRAP's Application Process page says locally manufactured products are applied for on Form 3 and imported products on Form 5, each with a processing fee. — [DRAP Application Process](https://www.dra.gov.pk/?p=1092) (snippet). The HOTC guidance says product enlistment is "Form 7, within 90 days", and that a Form 6 holder applies on the relevant form. — [DRAP HOTC guidelines](https://www.dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/HOTC-guidelines.pdf) (snippet). The SRO's Schedule A divides product applications by claim type: traditional-use, nutritional and structure-function claims go on Form 3; disease-risk-reduction or therapeutic claims go on Form 4. — [SRO 412(I)/2014](https://www.dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/SRO412-I-2014-Alternative-Medicines.pdf) (snippet). *Likely reconciliation:* Forms 1 to 5 are applications and Forms 6 to 8 are the certificates issued (6 = firm, 7 = product, 8 = contract manufacturing). Verify this.
- **Fees:**
  - DRAP's "Revised Regulatory Fee for Therapeutic Goods" (01.08.2025) lists alternative medicine and health product enlistment lines with amounts of **PKR 3,100 and 6,300**, and **PKR 10,400 and 19,900** for site and manufacturer applications. The PDF's text extraction scrambles the columns, so which amount belongs to which line is unconfirmed. — [DRAP fee PDF Aug 2025](https://www.dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Revised-DRAP-Regulatory-Fee-for-Therapeutic-Goods-01.08.2025.pdf) (snippet)
  - An earlier consolidated schedule (17.04.2023) shows "10,000 per product" near a contract-firm enlistment line, again with ambiguous layout. — [DRAP 2023 fee PDF](https://www.dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/DRAP-Consolidated-Fee-17.04.2023.pdf); see also [DRAP fee 30.08.2024](https://www.dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/DRAP-Regulatory-Fee-30.08.2024.pdf)
  - TIPP (older figures): alternative medicine PKR 2,500 and health product PKR 5,000 per product. — [TIPP](https://tipp.gov.pk/index.php?r=searchProcedure/view1&id=60)
  - A 2014 report: company enlistment fee Rs 10,000. — [Daily Times](https://dailytimes.com.pk/49713/drap-bans-import-of-alternative-medicine-health-products/)
  - Search results also mention a separate DRAP notification on regulatory fees dated March 2026, but its contents were not retrieved. A DRAP committee guidance note mentions a fee of three times the current fee in some cases (context unclear). — [DRAP committee guidance](https://www.dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/General-Guidance-for-Regulatory-Practices-on-Recommendation-by-Committee.pdf)
- **Timeline:** DRAP's HOTC guidance targets product enlistment (Form 7) "within 90 days". — [HOTC guidelines](https://www.dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/HOTC-guidelines.pdf)
- **Establishment licence:** DRAP has a separate "Alternative Medicines & Health Products Establishments" licensing and inspection page (Health/OTC establishment licence). — [DRAP establishment licence page](https://www.dra.gov.pk/therapeutic-goods/licensing-and-inspections/health-otc-products-establishment-license/)
- **Advertising:** the SRO text indicates that advertisements need prior permission from the Authority after it reviews the content. — [SRO 412(I)/2014](https://www.dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/SRO412-I-2014-Alternative-Medicines.pdf) (snippet). Secondary summaries say no unapproved claims to cure, treat or prevent disease are allowed. — [LinkedIn summary](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/alternative-medicines-health-products-enlistment-dr-); [SlideShare lecture](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/drap-alternative-medicines-and-health-products-enlistment-rules-2014-lecture-4-pptx/286398376)
- **Labelling:** in a pharmacist survey, 78.1% rated the enlistment number on the pack as important, which implies it is expected on labels. — [IJDRA study](https://www.ijdra.com/index.php/journal/article/download/737/392). The rules' labelling schedule could not be retrieved.

### Inferences
- **Typical documents (inferred from DRAP checklists and general practice; verify):** firm documents (NTN, SECP certificate or partnership deed, CNICs), the manufacturer's establishment licence and GMP evidence, the contract-manufacturing agreement (for rule 6), product formula and composition per dose, source and specification of raw shilajit, a certificate of analysis (COA) for the finished product, stability data supporting the claimed shelf life, label and pack artwork, the list of claims with supporting evidence (traditional-use references), and the fee challan.
- **Labelling (best practice consistent with DRAP Health & OTC practice; verify against the SRO schedule):** product and brand name; DRAP enlistment number; composition/quantity per serving; batch/lot number; manufacturing and expiry dates; name and address of manufacturer and marketer (including the contract manufacturer); net weight; dosage and directions; storage; warnings (pregnancy, children, medical conditions, "consult a physician"); and a "not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease" style disclaimer for health products. Urdu labelling may also be expected.
- **Claims:** avoid "cures impotence", "Viagra alternative", "treats diabetes" and similar wording. Structure-function or traditional-use wording ("traditionally used to support energy and vitality") is the low-risk tier (Form 3 type). Sexual-enhancement marketing is exactly what DRAP's 2025 aphrodisiac crackdown targets (see section 7).
- **Penalties:** DRAP Act 2012 / Drugs Act 1976 penalties for selling unregistered or misbranded therapeutic goods include fines, seizure and prosecution. Exact amounts were not verified in this session.
- **Realistic DRAP timeline:** 90 days is the official target. Industry practice suggests 3 to 9 months including queries (not sourced). Budget about 6 months.

### Gaps
- Exact current per-product fee (August 2025 or March 2026 schedule) not confirmed.
- Full labelling clauses and the exact advertising-approval procedure not retrieved.
- Whether a pure trader (no factory) can hold enlistment under rule 6 without its own QC lab needs confirming with DRAP or a consultant.

## 3. Business registration: proprietorship vs SECP, FBR/NTN/STRN, provincial licences, trademark, export

### Takeaway
A sole proprietor needs only an FBR NTN (free on IRIS), while a SECP single-member company gives limited liability and gets its NTN automatically. A trademark filing (TM-1) costs about PKR 12,000 per class (Chambers 2025/26) and takes 12 to 18 months to register. A Punjab Food Authority licence fee is calculated online from business size. Sales tax registration (STRN) depends on turnover and province.

### Cited Findings
- A sole proprietorship is not registered with SECP. The owner registers with FBR for an NTN, and may get a local or chamber business licence. — [startup.pk / guide aggregation](https://startup.pk/how-to-register-a-business-with-secp-in-pakistan-2026/); [invest.gov.pk](https://www.invest.gov.pk/starting-business)
- The SECP incorporation fee depends on authorised capital. Guides cite about PKR 1,000 filing fee for capital of PKR 100,000 to 500,000 (sources conflict; check SECP's fee calculator). — [startup.pk 2025](https://startup.pk/how-to-legally-register-your-startup-in-pakistan-with-secp-2025/); [TechJuice guide](https://www.techjuice.pk/guides/?p=759)
- A single-member company (Companies Act 2017 s.2(63)) needs one director/shareholder. Companies incorporated via SECP eServices get an NTN through the SECP–FBR integration. — [ICT blog 2026](https://www.ict.edu.pk/blogs/company-registration-and-compliance-in-pakistan-2026); [ICT SECP vs FBR](https://www.ict.edu.pk/blogs/secp-vs-fbr-pakistan-differences-filing-guide-2026)
- **Trademark:** TM-1 filing with an official fee of PKR 12,000 (Chambers Trade Marks & Copyright 2025 and 2026). Other sources give lower, older figures (PKR 3,000 per class in 2019; one 2026 blog says PKR 6,000 plus 4,000 per extra class). — [Chambers 2025](https://gpg-pdf.chambers.com/view/100585121/420); [Chambers 2026](https://gpg-pdf.chambers.com/Trade-Marks-and-Copyright-2026/375/); [S.S. Rana fee notice](https://ssrana.in/articles/pakistan-government-notifies-revised-fee-structure-for-trademarks/); [blog](https://sohaibnsultan.blogspot.com/2026/04/legal-process-to-register-brand-logo.html)
- Trademark timeline: 12 to 18 months from TM-1 to certificate (TM-11). — [Baco calculator](https://www.bacoconsultants.com/calculators/trademark-registration); [Kaizen 2020](https://kaizencpa.com/download/tm/Pakistan%20Trademark%20Registration%20Costs%20and%20Procedures%20(2020).pdf)
- **Punjab Food Authority:** single online form; the fee is calculated from business category, investment, sales, rent, staff, location and bills. The PFA sends an SMS for the fee, payable at Allied Bank or Easypaisa after inspection. A licence can be applied for only once the business starts. 2024 news examples: small restaurant Rs 10,000 to 20,000. — [PFA Resource & Licensing](https://pfa.gop.pk/wings/resource-licensing); [ARY News](https://arynews.tv/punjab-food-authority-license-fee-update-for-single-branch-restaurant-dec-2024)
- **Export to the US:** FDA Import Alert 54-17 allows detention without physical examination of dietary supplements contaminated with lead, arsenic, mercury and/or cadmium. Import alerts 66-41 and 99-42 have been used for Ayurvedic products with heavy metals. — [FDA Ayurvedic heavy-metals page](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/information-consumers-and-patients-drugs/fda-warns-about-heavy-metal-poisoning-associated-certain-unapproved-ayurvedic-drug-products); [Import Alert 54-17 doc](https://hbw.pharmaintelligence.informa.com/-/media/pmbi-old-site/supporting-documents/the-tan-sheet/24/08/0816-import-alert-5417.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Recommended structure:** a sole proprietorship is cheapest to start (NTN free on FBR IRIS, same day). An SMC (roughly PKR 5,000 to 15,000 all-in DIY, more through an agent; unverified) is better if you want limited liability, investors or easier marketplace/payment-gateway onboarding.
- **Sales tax:** packaged branded goods sold online usually attract provincial or federal sales tax (18% federal on goods under the Sales Tax Act 1990). E-commerce sellers above FBR's turnover thresholds and online marketplaces face withholding rules. Have a tax adviser confirm STRN registration timing. *Not verified in this session.*
- **Sindh Food Authority** issues a similar licence for Karachi-based operations if a food licence applies. Fee not found.
- **Exporting:** the US treats shilajit as a dietary supplement, which requires FDA facility registration, labelling under DSHEA/21 CFR 101.36, cGMP (21 CFR 111) and no disease claims. Heavy-metal failures lead to import alerts. California Prop 65 lead warnings apply. In the UK, shilajit may be treated as a novel food unless a history of consumption is established; check with the UK FSA. These points come from general knowledge and were *not sourced in this session*.

## 4. Lab tests: what to test, standards and limits, Pakistani labs and costs

### Takeaway
A credible batch COA should cover identity and quality (fulvic acid by a stated method, humic acid, moisture, ash, water solubility, pH, and optionally dibenzo-alpha-pyrones), heavy metals (Pb, As, Hg, Cd by ICP-MS/ICP-OES/AAS), microbiology (TPC, yeast and mould, E. coli, Salmonella, S. aureus), mycotoxins (aflatoxins), and adulteration screening (sildenafil/tadalafil, added fillers). No shilajit-specific Pakistani standard was found. Use WHO, USP or AYUSH/FSSAI limits as references. Pakistani PNAC-accredited labs (ICCBS-IAC Karachi, PCSIR, SGS Pakistan and others) can do these tests, but none of them publishes price lists.

### Cited Findings
- AYUSH heavy-metal maximums for finished Ayurvedic products, as reported by one clinical site: Pb under 10 ppm, As under 3 ppm, Hg under 1 ppm, Cd under 0.3 ppm. Premium vendor specs are much tighter (Pb under 0.5, As under 1, Cd under 0.2, Hg under 0.1 ppm). One vendor COA cites USP-style limits (total heavy metals not more than 10 ppm, Hg not more than 0.5 ppm). — [Drlogy 5-marker framework](https://www.drlogy.com/health/shilajit-safety-profile-5-marker-verification-framework); [Herbojit lab report](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0892/8038/1207/files/HERBOJIT_SHILAJIT_GOLD_LAB_REPORT.pdf) (the AYUSH figures were not verified against the official gazette)
- FSSAI Health Supplements Regulations (2022) are said to set heavy-metal limits for shilajit sold in India, with inconsistent enforcement. "Purified" is an unregulated label. — [MedBound Times](https://www.medboundtimes.com/fitness-and-wellness/shilajit-benefits-testosterone-safety-heavy-metal-risks)
- Fulvic acid: good purified resin contains about 15 to 20% fulvic acid by weight, while concentrated extract powders are standardised to 50 to 60% or higher. Methods (HPLC vs gravimetric/titrimetric) are not equivalent, so the COA must state the method. One report found brand fulvic content varied by about 32,000% (secondary claim). — [ContractLaboratory](https://contractlaboratory.com/shilajit-purity-testing/); [MedBound Times](https://www.medboundtimes.com/fitness-and-wellness/shilajit-benefits-testosterone-safety-heavy-metal-risks); [Green Jeeva 40% extract](https://www.greenjeeva.com/product/shilajit-extract-powder-40-fulvic-acid/1803)
- Shilajit products have failed heavy-metal checks before. A Syncom "Shilajit" was among the 14 Ayurvedic products Health Canada flagged after the 2004 JAMA study for potentially harmful lead, mercury or arsenic. The 2008 JAMA study found about 20% of Ayurvedic products bought online contained lead, mercury or arsenic. — [Health Canada 2005](https://www.canada.ca/en/news/archive/2005/03/some-ayurvedic-medicinal-products-reported-contain-high-levels-heavy-metals.html); [FDA 2008 archive](https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/eot2008/20090109013345/http://www.fda.gov/consumer/updates/ayurvedic101608.html)
- Raw shilajit carries mycotoxin and microbial risk (fungal contamination). — [ResearchGate review](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/356505030_Review_on_Shilajit_Used_as_Pakistani_Traditional_Medicine)
- **ICCBS Industrial Analytical Centre (Univ. of Karachi):** PNAC-accredited (LAB-039, scope revised Jan 2026) for food, water and pharmaceutical testing, including metals (Pb, Cd and others). Serves more than 500 organisations. No public rate card. — [PNAC LAB-39 scope](https://www.pnac.gov.pk/pdfFiles/LAB-39.pdf)
- **SGS Pakistan** offers food-contaminant testing (heavy metals, mycotoxins, pesticides). — [SGS Pakistan](https://www.sgs.com/en-pk/service-groups/food-contaminants)
- **LUMS Central Lab (Lahore)** publishes ICP-OES external pricing for 2026, with industry charged twice the academic rate. — [LUMS ICP-OES pricing 2026](https://centrallab.lums.edu.pk/sites/default/files/2026-05/ICP-OES_pricing_external_users_2026.pdf)
- A list of PNAC-accredited labs is available on PNAC's site. — [PNAC accredited testing labs](https://www.pnac.gov.pk/TestingandCalibrationLaboratoies/Active?page=4)
- **Reference price (not Pakistani):** Saudi SFDA charges 130 SAR per element for heavy metals. — [SFDA test prices](https://SFDA.GOV.SA/sites/default/files/2023-10/Tests-Prices.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Recommended test panel per batch** (draws on the sources above plus standard supplement QC practice):
  1. Heavy metals Pb, As, Hg, Cd (ICP-MS preferred) against limits of about Pb ≤ 10, As ≤ 3, Hg ≤ 1, Cd ≤ 0.3 ppm (AYUSH-type) as a minimum. Aim for US/EU-export grade (Pb ≤ 1 to 3 ppm, roughly; WHO herbal guidance Pb 10, Cd 0.3 mg/kg) if you plan to export.
  2. Microbiology: TPC (for example ≤10^4 to 10^5 cfu/g), yeast and mould (≤10^3 cfu/g), E. coli absent/g, Salmonella absent/25 g, S. aureus absent. Ranges follow USP <2023>/WHO herbal norms and should be verified.
  3. Aflatoxins B1/total (for example B1 ≤ 5 µg/kg, total ≤ 10 µg/kg; EU-style; verify).
  4. Fulvic acid % (state the method) and humic acid %, moisture/loss on drying, total ash, acid-insoluble ash (sand or soil adulteration), water solubility, pH.
  5. Adulteration: sildenafil/tadalafil screening by HPLC/LC-MS (important given DRAP's aphrodisiac crackdown). Optional: DBPs (dibenzo-alpha-pyrones) and FTIR fingerprint for authenticity.
- **Testing frequency:** do a full panel on the first batches and for DRAP enlistment. After that, test heavy metals and microbiology on every batch and the full panel at least once a year or whenever the raw-material source changes.
- **Costs (estimate, unsourced):** a full panel at a Pakistani PNAC-accredited lab might be roughly PKR 25,000 to 60,000 per sample, depending on ICP-MS vs AAS and on adulterant screening. Get written quotes from ICCBS-IAC, PCSIR (Lahore/Karachi/Peshawar), SGS Pakistan, Intertek Pakistan, NIH Islamabad, and the University of Agriculture Faisalabad (NIFSAT). Gilgit-Baltistan sourcing makes PCSIR Peshawar or Islamabad labs convenient.

### Gaps
- No published PKR price list was found for PCSIR, NIH, ICCBS, SGS or Intertek Pakistan.
- No Pakistani (PSQCA/DRAP) monograph for shilajit was found. The official FSSAI/AYUSH numeric limits were not verified from primary gazettes.

## 5. Halal certification options and cost

### Takeaway
The Pakistan Halal Authority (PHA, under the PHA Act 2016) licenses the halal mark, while PNAC-accredited and PHA-registered private certification bodies issue certificates. In 2025 the PHA cut the halal-mark fee to zero for exports and by 50% for local food items. I found no published PKR amounts.

### Cited Findings

## 6. Recent DRAP actions and warnings on fake or adulterated shilajit or sex-enhancement products

### Takeaway
I found no DRAP alert that names shilajit. However, DRAP issued several 2025 rapid alerts against unregistered or falsified aphrodisiacs sold online and on social media, and Punjab flagged "herbal" products spiked with sildenafil. This is the enforcement environment a shilajit seller positioned for "male vitality" operates in.

### Cited Findings
