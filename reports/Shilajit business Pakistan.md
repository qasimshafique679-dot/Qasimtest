# Win Pakistan's shilajit market with proof, not price

Pakistan's shilajit market has three kinds of seller. The old tibb houses (Hamdard, Ajmal, Qarshi and the dawakhanas) sell salajeet only as a cheap ingredient in Rs 50–750 tablets and majoons. Established Gilgit-Baltistan, Hunza and Chitral brands (Hunza Foodways, Organic Aprico, Chitral House, Chiltan Pure, plus Hemani from the FMCG side) sell pure resin at about **Rs 118–150 per gram**. Fifteen or more newer D2C brands, most on Shopify (Taj Sultan, Original Shilajit PK, Vitalis Living, Organicles and others), now compete mainly on **published lab reports, cash on delivery, free delivery and SEO**. The opening for a newcomer is a trust gap. No resin brand found shows a DRAP enlistment number, Daraz is full of unrated "Gilgit 10g" listings, and the brands that publish PCSIR or Eurofins reports lead the comparison content. Legally, packaged shilajit sold for health or vigour most likely needs **DRAP Health & OTC enlistment** under SRO 412(I)/2014. The realistic way in for a startup is a DRAP-licensed contract manufacturer, a batch lab panel (heavy metals, microbes, aflatoxin, fulvic acid and sildenafil/tadalafil screening) and marketing that never makes sexual-performance claims. That last rule matters because DRAP ran an aphrodisiac crackdown in 2025. The economics are tighter than the retail prices suggest. If you buy purified resin at wholesale prices and pay Pakistani Meta acquisition costs, a single 20g jar loses money on the first order. A viable plan therefore depends on cheap sourcing near the origin, 40–60g bundles, WhatsApp-driven repeat orders and higher-margin formats such as gummies. A lean launch costs roughly **PKR 0.35–0.4 million**. A comfortable launch costs about **PKR 1.4–1.6 million** and takes about six months, mostly because of DRAP.

A note on the evidence: researchers could not open most brand sites, daraz.pk or dra.gov.pk directly. Prices below come from search-indexed snippets dated mostly October–November 2026. Much of the cross-brand comparison material was published by Taj Sultan, a competitor that ranks itself first. Check live prices and DRAP fees before you commit money.

## Three tiers of brands, and only the newest compete hard

### The old pharma houses sell salajeet as an ingredient, not as resin

The oldest names are old companies, not old shilajit brands. **Hamdard Pakistan** was set up as Hamdard Laboratories (Waqf) in 1948 from a Delhi dawakhana founded in 1906 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamdard_Pakistan)). **Dawakhana Hakim Ajmal Khan** dates to about 1949 in Lahore, although sources disagree on its founder ([LinkedIn](https://pk.linkedin.com/company/dhak); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hakim_Ajmal_Khan)). **Qarshi** descends from Hakim Muhammad Hassan Qarshi's dawakhana ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hakim_Muhammad_Hassan_Qarshi)). None of them was found selling pure resin. Their salajeet products are low-dose Unani compounds sold through pharmacies and superstores. Hamdard's Qurs Salajeet sells over the counter for Rs 50–80 per 20 tablets, and one pharmacy reported it "temporarily unavailable from manufacturer due to manufacturing issues" ([Sehat](https://sehat.com.pk/products/Qurs-Salajeet-20qurs.html); [Oladoc](https://oladoc.com/pharmacy/medicine/hamdard-qurs-salajeet-tablet)). Ajmal positions Akseer Salajeet for bones and joints, not for sexual health ([Ajmal.pk](https://www.ajmal.pk/products/akseer-salajeet)). The research found no salajeet product at Marhaba Laboratories and no current product page at Qarshi. No large nutraceutical brand (Nutrifactor, Sunny Health, Getz) surfaced with a shilajit product either. **Pure resin therefore has no legacy pharma leader**, and that leaves the category open.

## DRAP enlistment and a five-part lab panel are the real entry ticket

### Shilajit falls under DRAP's Health & OTC division, not just a food licence

Packaged shilajit sold with health or "strength" positioning most likely counts as a **health product (nutraceutical)** under the Alternative Medicines and Health Products (Enlistment) Rules 2014, **SRO 412(I)/2014**, which DRAP's Health & OTC (non-drugs) division administers ([DRAP SRO](https://www.dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/SRO412-I-2014-Alternative-Medicines.pdf); [HOTC guidelines](https://www.dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/HOTC-guidelines.pdf)). Both the firm and each product must be enlisted before sale. Supplements receive an *enlistment* number, which is different from a drug *registration* number ([Pakera](https://pakera.pk/drap-clarifies-sro-1688-probiotics-supplements-no-new-category/)). Labelling the product as a Unani remedy moves it into the "alternative medicine" path. Making disease or impotence claims risks it being treated as a drug, and full drug registration is out of reach for a startup. No official ruling that classifies shilajit specifically was found. A provincial food licence (Punjab or Sindh Food Authority) would apply only if the product were sold as a plain food with no health claims, and that is rarely how shilajit is sold, so **treat DRAP as the default** and confirm the classification with a consultant.

The practical route is the **contract-giver model**. The rules allow "contract givers under rule 6" to hold enlistment, with a Form 8 contract-manufacturing certificate ([DRAP SRO](https://www.dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/SRO412-I-2014-Alternative-Medicines.pdf)). You hire a DRAP-licensed Health & OTC manufacturer to pack your resin. You then either enlist the product in that manufacturer's name and act as marketer, or enlist your own firm as contract giver and enlist the product under your brand. Forms are reported inconsistently. Forms 1–2 enlist a firm as manufacturer or importer. Form 3 covers traditional-use, nutritional and structure-function product claims, and Form 4 covers therapeutic claims. Forms 6–8 appear to be the certificates DRAP issues ([DRAP Form 1](https://www.dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/12.-Application-form-1-for-enlistment-of-firm-as-manufacturer.docx); [DRAP Application Process](https://www.dra.gov.pk/?p=1092)). Fees are modest. DRAP's August 2025 schedule shows lines of about **PKR 3,100 and 6,300** for product enlistment and **PKR 10,400–19,900** for site or manufacturer applications, although the PDF's columns could not be matched to lines with certainty ([DRAP fee PDF](https://www.dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Revised-DRAP-Regulatory-Fee-for-Therapeutic-Goods-01.08.2025.pdf)). The fees are not the bottleneck; time is. DRAP's own target is **90 days** for product enlistment ([HOTC guidelines](https://www.dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/HOTC-guidelines.pdf)), but more than **30,000 enlistment applications** were reportedly pending at the Health & OTC directorate in late 2025 ([ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2025/09/24/tip-slams-drap-for-failing-to-regulate-alternative-medicines-despite-court-orders/amp/)). Plan for about six months.

Advertising is regulated too. DRAP's January 2025 advertisement rules require ad claims to match the claims approved at enlistment ([DRAP Advertisement rules](https://dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Advertisement-rules.pdf)). In February 2026 DRAP warned companies that every listed product needs **Advertisement Board clearance** before it is advertised, and singled out "complete cure / guaranteed treatment" claims ([ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/02/12/drap-warns-companies-over-unapproved-medicine-advertisements/amp/)). Enforcement is real. In 2025 DRAP issued rapid alerts against unregistered sildenafil tablets sold "online and on social media" (12 September). It followed with a crackdown on falsified aphrodisiacs in Alert No. 98 (22 October) and ordered supply-chain confiscations in Alert No. 125 (30 December) ([DRAP alert](https://www.dra.gov.pk/?p=77952); [Rapid Alert 98](https://www.dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/98.-Rapid-Alert-Aphrodiasic.pdf); [Rapid Alert 125](https://www.dra.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/125.-Rapid-Alert-Aphrodiasic.pdf)). Punjab separately flagged "herbal" majoons spiked with sildenafil and sold online ([ARY News](https://arynews.tv/punjab-issues-alert-on-dangerous-herbal-products)). None of these alerts names shilajit. Even so, a brand positioned on "mardana taqat" is advertising in exactly the category regulators are targeting.

To be fair to the other side: many competitors clearly sell without visible enlistment and have not been stopped, so launching first and enlisting later is a common gamble. The cost of that gamble is rising, though. Without enlistment you also lose Daraz Mall eligibility, the claims defence it gives you, and the trust advantage a printed DRAP number would provide in a market full of fakes.

### Business registration, trademark and halal are cheap and quick

A sole proprietor needs only a free FBR NTN. A SECP single-member company gives limited liability and receives its NTN automatically through SECP–FBR integration ([invest.gov.pk](https://www.invest.gov.pk/starting-business); [ICT](https://www.ict.edu.pk/blogs/company-registration-and-compliance-in-pakistan-2026)). Payment gateways may require formal registration and exact name matches across documents ([assanpay](https://assanpay.com/payment-gateway-setup-fees-pakistan/)), which favours an SMC once you plan to accept cards. File a **trademark (TM-1)** in class 5 on day one. The official fee is about **PKR 12,000 per class**, and registration takes **12–18 months** ([Chambers 2026](https://gpg-pdf.chambers.com/Trade-Marks-and-Copyright-2026/375/); [Baco](https://www.bacoconsultants.com/calculators/trademark-registration)). Daraz Mall's authenticity programme, launched in November 2025, accepts only verified brand partners ([ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2025/11/05/darazmall-introduces-authenticity-guarantee-ahead-of-11-11-to-elevate-brand-shopping-experience/amp/)). Halal certification is optional for domestic sales but useful for marketing and Gulf exports. The Pakistan Halal Authority launched its mark scheme in April 2025, set the halal-mark fee to zero for exports and **cut it by 50% for local food items** ([PHA Newsletter](https://pakistanhalalauthority.gov.pk/Files/PHA%20Newsletter%20V2%202025.pdf); [24 News](https://24newshd.tv/17-Apr-2025/halal-certification-mark-scheme-officially-launched)). Note one tax detail: Finance Act 2025 introduced **2% withholding on COD collections versus 1% on digital payments** ([theneoapps](https://theneoapps.com/blogs/shopify-cash-on-delivery-pakistan-setup-guide-cod-form-2026/)).

### Test every batch for metals, microbes, aflatoxin, identity and hidden drugs

No Pakistani shilajit standard was found, so use WHO, USP and AYUSH limits as the benchmark. AYUSH-type ceilings for finished products are reported as **lead under 10 ppm, arsenic under 3, mercury under 1 and cadmium under 0.3 ppm**. Premium vendors set much tighter limits, such as lead under 0.5 ppm and arsenic under 1 ppm ([Drlogy](https://www.drlogy.com/health/shilajit-safety-profile-5-marker-verification-framework)). The risk is real: a "Shilajit" product was among the Ayurvedic products Health Canada flagged for heavy metals in 2005 ([Health Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/news/archive/2005/03/some-ayurvedic-medicinal-products-reported-contain-high-levels-heavy-metals.html)). Raw shilajit also carries fungal and mycotoxin risk ([ResearchGate review](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/356505030_Review_on_Shilajit_Used_as_Pakistani_Traditional_Medicine)). Good purified resin contains about **15–20% fulvic acid**, and results differ by test method, so the COA must name the method ([ContractLaboratory](https://contractlaboratory.com/shilajit-purity-testing/)). Note that Organic Aprico's 73% claim sits far above this range, and the method behind it is not stated.

| Test group | What to test | Reference limit (verify) |
|---|---|---|
| Heavy metals | Lead, arsenic, mercury, cadmium by ICP-MS/ICP-OES | Pb ≤10, As ≤3, Hg ≤1, Cd ≤0.3 ppm minimum; much tighter for export |
| Microbiology | Total plate count, yeast and mould, E. coli, Salmonella, S. aureus | TPC ≤10⁴–10⁵ cfu/g; yeast and mould ≤10³; pathogens absent |
| Mycotoxins | Aflatoxin B1 and total | B1 ≤5 µg/kg, total ≤10 µg/kg (EU-style) |
| Identity and quality | Fulvic and humic acid % (state the method), moisture, total ash, acid-insoluble ash, water solubility, pH | ~15–20% fulvic for purified resin |
| Adulteration | Sildenafil and tadalafil screening by HPLC/LC-MS; optional FTIR fingerprint | Not detected |

The limits are the research team's synthesis of the sources above. Run the full panel on your first batches and for DRAP enlistment, then test metals, microbiology and adulterants on every batch. Labs that can do this work include **ICCBS-IAC Karachi** (PNAC-accredited LAB-039), **SGS Pakistan**, PCSIR's Lahore, Karachi and Peshawar labs, and **LUMS Central Lab**, which publishes 2026 ICP-OES pricing with industry charged twice the academic rate ([PNAC LAB-39](https://www.pnac.gov.pk/pdfFiles/LAB-39.pdf); [SGS Pakistan](https://www.sgs.com/en-pk/service-groups/food-contaminants); [LUMS](https://centrallab.lums.edu.pk/sites/default/files/2026-05/ICP-OES_pricing_external_users_2026.pdf)). None of the other labs publishes a full-panel price. The research team's estimate of **PKR 25,000–60,000 per sample** is unsourced, so get written quotes. Your label should carry the enlistment number, batch, manufacture and expiry dates, composition, net weight, manufacturer and marketer details, directions, warnings and a "not intended to diagnose, treat or cure" disclaimer. A QR code linking to the batch COA turns the compliance work into marketing.

## The unit economics only work with cheap sourcing and repeat buyers

Wholesale purified resin lists at **PKR 55,000–95,000 per kg** in 2026 ([Chitral House blog](https://chitralhouse.com/blogs/news/shilajit-1kg-price-in-pakistan); [Hunza Foodways](https://hunzafoodways.com/shop/pure-himalayan-salajeet-shilajit/); [Taj Sultan](https://tajsultan.com/collections/shilajit-in-pakistan)). Private-label wholesale goes lower, to Chitral House's 60g packs at about **Rs 47/g** (MOQ 100) ([chaicon](https://www.chaicon.com/chitralhouse)). One raw, unprocessed 40 kg lot from Gilgit-Baltistan is listed at about **PKR 6,250/kg**. That is an outlier to verify, and raw rock loses heavily in purification ([GBN Mart](https://www.gbnmart.com/raw-shilajit-from-gilgit-baltistan-natural-unprocessed-himalayan-shilajit-bulk-wholesale-supply-40kg)). Sellers describe the same purification method: dissolve in water, settle, filter, then sun-dry for 40–90 days ([Organic Aprico](https://organicaprico.com/); [Sulaiman Aftabi](https://www.sulaiman-aftabi.com/about)). An academic study confirms that the drying method changes the product's properties ([IJATM](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/377380892_International_Journal_of_Ayurveda_and_Traditional_Medicine_A_Comparative_study_of_Physio-chemical_Properties_and_Pharmaceutical_Process_of_Shilajit_Asphaltum_punja-bianum_Purification_with_Triphala_Kw)). Local amber glass jars cost **PKR 150–180** ([Bio Shop](https://bioshop.pk/products/amber-glass-cosmetic-jar-with-silver-lid)). The research team estimates full packaging (jar, spoon, label, box, mailer) at about PKR 225–385 per unit. A 0.5 kg parcel costs PKR 150–250 to ship, plus a 1–3% COD fee ([trackmyorder.pk](https://trackmyorder.pk/blog/guides/postex-courier-rates-2026); [lcstracking](https://www.lcstracking.pk/delivery-cost-calculator/)).

On the demand side, a Pakistani Shopify brand reported blended **CAC of PKR 2,240–3,150** ([WeProms](https://weproms.com/case-studies/shopify-subscription-attach-roas-case-study/)). National COD return-to-origin (RTO) runs about **18–20%**, and roughly 1 in 5 parcels came back in a study of about 170,000 orders ([DHL Pakistan](https://www.dhl.com/discover/en-pk/e-commerce-advice/e-commerce-best-practice/subscription-marketing/how-pakistani-e-commerce-sellers-can-reduce-cod-returns); [Ordernation](https://www.ordernation.com/blogs/research/state-of-online-shopping-pakistan-2026)). Combining these sourced inputs gives the following first-order economics. The table rounds packaging to Rs 300 and shipping to Rs 200 plus a 2% COD fee, and adds the 2% COD withholding tax.

| Scenario | Price | Resin cost | Packaging + shipping + COD fee + 2% WHT | Contribution before ads | Paid CAC per delivered order |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20g jar, private-label resin at Rs 47/g | 2,499 | 940 | ~600 | **~960** | ~1,900–4,400 |
| 20g jar, wholesale resin at Rs 85/g | 2,499 | 1,700 | ~600 | **~200** | ~1,900–4,400 |
| 60g bundle at Rs 47/g | 5,499 | 2,820 | ~720 | **~1,960** | ~1,900–4,400 |
| 60g bundle at Rs 85/g | 5,499 | 5,100 | ~720 | **~-320** | ~1,900–4,400 |

The table supports three conclusions. First, **a single 20g jar sold through paid ads loses money on the first order** at any realistic sourcing cost, so the business makes its money on the second and third jars. Shilajit is a 30–60 day consumable, which suits WhatsApp reorders and subscriptions. Second, **buying purified resin at Rs 85–95/g from other brands and reselling it cannot work**. You need to buy at or below about Rs 50/g, through direct Gilgit-Baltistan or Chitral relationships, private-label wholesale, or in-house purification of semi-purified resin. Third, **the AOV must sit at Rs 4,500+**. That means leading with 40–60g "course" bundles, as Chitral House and Original Shilajit PK do, and setting free delivery at about Rs 3,000, just above a single jar. Gummies and honey sachets have the best gross margins, but they require a contract manufacturer. Exports show where the real margin is: PakShilajit sells 15g on Amazon US for **$70 (about $4.67/g)**, roughly ten times the domestic price per gram ([Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/PakShilajit-Himalayan-Shilajit-Purified-Essential/dp/B0CP2V2WQS)). Export, however, carries its own FDA hurdle. FDA Import Alert 54-17 allows detention of supplements contaminated with heavy metals ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/information-consumers-and-patients-drugs/fda-warns-about-heavy-metal-poisoning-associated-certain-unapproved-ayurvedic-drug-products)).

## A six-month launch plan from registration to first reorder

### Steps 1–2 (weeks 1–6): register, trademark and lock in sourcing

Register as a sole proprietor (free NTN) or SMC and open a business bank account. File TM-1 in class 5 the same week, because the 12–18 month clock gates Daraz Mall. Next, collect samples from four or five Gilgit-Baltistan and Chitral suppliers. Ask each for a batch heavy-metal report before paying, as buying guides recommend ([Chitral Herbs](https://chitralherbs.com/blogs/news/top-10-shilajit-buying-tips-for-pakistan)). Send your two best samples for a quick metals and sildenafil screen. Target landed resin cost at or below Rs 50/g. At the same time, shortlist DRAP-licensed Health & OTC contract manufacturers, check their establishment licences on DRAP's licensing page ([DRAP](https://www.dra.gov.pk/therapeutic-goods/licensing-and-inspections/health-otc-products-establishment-license/)), and hire a regulatory consultant to confirm the current forms, fees and classification.

### Steps 3–4 (months 2–6): enlist with DRAP while building the Shopify store

File firm and product enlistment through the contract-giver route, attaching the full-panel COA, label artwork and only structure-function or traditional-use claims ("traditionally used to support energy and vitality"). Build the store in parallel. Shopify's Pakistan pricing page shows **Basic at $19/month after a 3-day trial and $1/month for 3 months**. Grow is $49 and Advanced $299. You pay in USD with an international-enabled Pakistani card ([Shopify PK](https://www.shopify.com/pk-ur/pricing); [Shopify Community](https://community.shopify.com/t/credit-debit-card-requirements-from-pakistan/115875)). **Shopify Payments is not available in Pakistan** ([xstak](https://www.xstak.com/blog/payment-gateways-in-pakistan)). A .pk domain costs **Rs 3,899/yr** ([HosterPK](https://blog.hosterpk.com/pk-domain-prices-update/)).

Set up COD as your main payment method. Add bank transfer and JazzCash or Easypaisa as manual options, then a card gateway (Safepay or PayFast, about 1.5–3%) once you are registered. On the Basic plan Shopify adds its own **2% fee on third-party gateway orders** ([loop2tech](https://loop2tech.com/blog/shopify-payment-gateway-pakistan)). For logistics, open two courier accounts: PostEx for fast COD payouts and Leopards (1% COD fee, minimum Rs 50) or TCS for reach. Book through **Courierify** (Pro $19.99/month, free plan available) or **ShipOps**, which adds Urdu call and WhatsApp confirmation ([Courierify](https://courierifyapp.com/); [ShipOps](https://shipops.app/shopify-cod-app-pakistan/)).

The rest of the store needs a free theme such as Dawn, a COD order form, WhatsApp order confirmation, a reviews app, and the Meta and TikTok pixels. Add policy pages and a "Lab Reports" page with batch COAs linked by QR from each jar. Photography costs PKR 2,000–6,000 per product in Lahore, or PKR 5,000–15,000 for a 10–20 image package in Karachi ([Hoop Studios](https://hoopstudios.com/blogs/product-photography/product-photography-cost-in-pakistan); [2288.pk](https://www.2288.pk/product-photography-in-karachi/)).

### Step 5 (launch, ideally October–February): sell purity, not potency

Winter is the natural peak, because "garam" tonics sell alongside the winter dry-fruit surge ([APP](https://www.app.com.pk/?p=1112642)). Pre-Ramadan stocking and Daraz's 11.11 sale add spikes, but Meta CPMs also rise in gifting seasons ([WeProms](https://weproms.com/case-studies/shopify-subscription-attach-roas-case-study/)). Pakistani Meta CPMs are among the cheapest in the world, at about **USD 0.60–2.90** ([Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/facebook-ads-cpm-by-country-2026); [Adamigo](https://www.adamigo.ai/blog/meta-ads-cpm-cpc-benchmarks-by-country-2026)). Plan for about 3x ROAS, not the 6–9x some agencies claim, which even their own source calls inflated ([Pixelmovers](https://pixelmovers.co/blog/meta-ads-roas-benchmarks-2026)). Start with a **PKR 50,000–100,000 test budget** ([WeProms](https://weproms-pk.pages.dev/blog/google-ads-pakistan-bidding-strategies-2026/)).

Keep ad copy to energy, vitality, Himalayan origin, fulvic acid and lab-tested purity. Meta allows sexual-wellness ads only when they focus on health rather than pleasure and target 18+, and TikTok applies similar rules ([Meta](https://transparency.meta.com/en-gb/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/health-wellness); [TikTok](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-healthcare-pharmaceuticals)). "Testosterone booster", "timing" or "mardana taqat" invite rejection and DRAP trouble. The testosterone evidence rests largely on one small 2016 trial anyway ([MDLinx](https://www.mdlinx.com/article/your-patients-are-using-shilajit-to-enhance-mental-clarity-and-improve-their-sex-life/3aZe2gOnmDz3vA4rb3PjxH)).

Build content around dissolve and burn tests, sourcing footage from Hunza and Skardu, and on-camera lab reports, because authenticity is buyers' main worry ([Skardu.pk](https://skardu.pk/how-to-test-the-authenticity-of-shilajit-salajeet-a-simple-guide/)). Brief micro-influencers (gym coaches, Gilgit-Baltistan travel vloggers) at **PKR 5,000–25,000 per post** on long-term, code-tracked deals ([WeProms](https://weproms-pk.pages.dev/blog/influencer-marketing-waste-pakistani-brands-roi/)). Write Urdu and Roman-Urdu SEO pages for "salajeet price in Pakistan", the battleground where Taj Sultan, Organic Aprico and Chitral House already compete. Confirm every COD order on WhatsApp within minutes. Merchants report RTO falling from 30–35% to the high teens when they do ([trackmyorder.pk](https://trackmyorder.pk/blog/shopify-tips/cod-return-rate-pakistan-shopify)). Offer a prepaid discount, which the 1% versus 2% withholding gap helps pay for.

### Step 6 (months 6–18): retain, extend and list on marketplaces

Run reorder reminders on WhatsApp at day 25, and offer a "3-month course" subscription. Add gummies or honey sachets through your contract manufacturer. List on regular Daraz, where the Health & Beauty commission is about 7–13% and all-in costs run 15–18% of revenue ([Scribd Daraz calculator](https://www.scribd.com/document/482319994/Daraz-Profit-Calculator-xlsx); [WeProms](https://weproms-pk.pages.dev/blog/ecommerce-hidden-costs-pakistan-profit-margins/)). Move to Daraz Mall once the trademark registers. Explore export only once your COAs meet US or EU heavy-metal limits.

## Conclusion

Shilajit in Pakistan looks like a commodity war over price per gram, but the data points elsewhere. Price per gram has already converged at about Rs 120 across the serious brands. The leaders in search, Taj Sultan and Original Shilajit PK, got there by publishing lab results, not by undercutting. The next step up the trust ladder is still empty: a resin brand with a printed DRAP enlistment number, a batch-specific COA behind a QR code and a clean sildenafil screen. In a market where buyers assume fakes and regulators are cracking down on aphrodisiacs, compliance is the brand. The six-month DRAP wait is a cost every serious entrant pays, and once paid it shuts out casual copycats.

The deeper lesson is that this is a **sourcing and retention business disguised as a marketing business**. Paid acquisition at Pakistani CACs cannot pay for a single jar. Your profit depends on whether you can buy resin at Rs 50/g or less, how quickly you can move customers onto 60g courses, and whether WhatsApp turns first-time buyers into quarterly reorders. Before you spend on ads, spend the first month on two tests: your landed resin cost and a lab report on it. Those two results decide whether the plan works.
